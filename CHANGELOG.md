# Changelog

## Unreleased

- Quick start: one screen, pick a look, tap Create my dashboard.
- Devices without an area are placed by name; homes with no areas get rooms from device names.
- Mix-and-match look: card shape, on-colour, navigation (tabs, side list, bottom dock), icons, titles, typeface.
- 19 backgrounds and photo upload.
- Hide or show the Home Assistant menu from the dashboard.

## 0.2.0

- New styles: Vivid (the new default), Apple Home style, Homey style and Minimal.
- Auto layout: one dashboard that adapts to phones, tablets and big screens.
- Home screen with status chips, favourites and rooms grouped by floor.
- Floors in the wizard, navigation and room settings; room wallpapers and all-lights button.
- Irrigation page with server-side zone timers, run-all, skip and stop, plus `dashforge.run_zones`, `dashforge.skip_zone` and `dashforge.stop_irrigation` actions.
- Live camera video, live 24-hour sensor graphs, weather, calendar and to-do cards.
- Water heaters, humidifiers, lawn mowers, remotes, counters, timers, text, updates, images, groups and device trackers.
- Per-card colour, tap action and "Show on Home".

## 0.1.0 – first release

- Setup wizard with ten steps: rooms, devices, scenes and media, header widgets, layout, look and feel, pages, wall tablet and review.
- Grid, Sidebar, List and Focus layouts.
- Edit mode: drag to reorder, hide, resize, rename, change icons, move cards between rooms, add devices, create rooms and pages.
- Cards for lights, switches, fans, covers, valves, climate, media players, locks, alarms, cameras, vacuums, sensors (with 24-hour history), numbers, selects, scenes, scripts and buttons.
- Header with greeting, clock, weather and forecast, calendar month view, reminders, scene strip and now-playing chip.
- Media, Security and Scenes pages plus custom pages.
- Themes: light, dark or automatic; accent colour; background presets or your own image; glass or solid cards; roundness; spacing.
- Wall tablet: screensaver, return to the first room, hide the Home Assistant sidebar.
- Live sync between devices, backup export and import.
